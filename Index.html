Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Do you pay more than the gold price?

Depending on the item, an offer may include value beyond the metal content, such as brand, gemstones, condition, craftsmanship and resale demand. Any premium is subject to assessment.

Do you pay for diamonds?

Eligible diamonds and gemstones are considered according to their characteristics, authenticity and resale potential.

Do you melt every item?

No promise should be made without assessing the piece. Items with potential designer, antique or collectible value may be considered for resale or preservation where appropriate.

Is the gold calculator a live price?

No. This prototype uses a demo rate that you can edit. A live data provider must be connected for real-time pricing.
